About DAWN

A home built around belonging.

Compassion, respect, supportive inclusion and cultural integrity guide how we care.

Who we are

People at the centre
of every decision.

DAWN Wellness Network is a residential support services provider for individuals with mental, physical, developmental and behavioural support needs.

Since 2022, DAWN has worked with social service organizations, including the Children’s Aid Society, to provide inclusive and supportive wellness services. Our home-based approach combines individualized staffing with care planning that respects each resident’s personal choices and rights.

Our home-based settings offer options for partnering agencies and continuity of care for residents.

Our vision

Space to heal,
grow and succeed.

To provide a safe, high standard of living through individualized, trauma-informed programs that improve residents’ lives and empower their growth.

Our mission

A deep sense of
value and community.

To create a future where every resident feels they belong, supported by a compassionate team and an environment that centres personal choice, independence and individual rights.

Meet the Director

Experience guided
by compassion.

Jacqueline P. Grant brings more than 25 years of experience in the social service sector.

Her work alongside social service agencies, including Peel Children’s Aid Society, has involved supporting people across diverse cultures, genders, mental health conditions and disabilities. That experience informs the framework behind DAWN Wellness Network.

“I have embodied a strong desire to assist in making a positive change in the lives of the clients I serve.”

Jacqueline welcomes conversations with agencies, societies and organizations seeking residential care services and support.
