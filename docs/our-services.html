Our services

Personalized support. Everyday possibility.

Individualized, trauma-informed support that honours each resident’s needs, choices and goals.

Residential supportIndividualized care planningMedication supportLife skills & programmingCommunity inclusionBehavioural & crisis support
01

Residential support

Individualized 1:1 support, or 2:1 staffing where necessary, delivered by a trained and experienced team in a supportive home environment.

  • Support matched to individual needs
  • A focus on choice and independence
  • Continuous, home-based support
02

Individualized care planning

Comprehensive intake and personal file management support an Individual Support Plan that is assessed and reviewed regularly with the resident.

  • Comprehensive intake
  • Personal goals and preferences
  • Regular plan review
03

Medication support

Medication administration and self-administration support, with blister-pack and as-needed (PRN) protocols, form part of the documented approach to resident support.

  • Medication administration
  • Self-administration support
  • Documented medication protocols
04

Life skills & programming

Residents help shape a weekly schedule that brings together wellness, personal interests and skill development. Activities reflect the person’s goals and abilities.

  • Yoga, fitness and wellness education
  • Cooking, baking, crafts and visual arts
  • Language, music and other skill development
05

Community inclusion

Supportive inclusion and cultural sensitivity guide opportunities for residents to engage with others and take part in community life.

  • Participation in community activities
  • Support toward personal goals
  • Respect for culture and identity
06

Behavioural & crisis support

Behavioural support plans are developed with residents and reviewed regularly. The approach uses the least intrusive, effective evidence-based practices, with CPI and/or UMAB-trained staffing.

  • Individualized behavioural support plans
  • Crisis prevention and response protocols
  • Regular review by staff

Wellness in everyday life

Weekly programming is created with each resident, around learning, interests, goals and wellness.

Meal planning considers Canada’s Food Guide and each resident’s cultural food preferences. Residents may prepare meals independently, cook with support, or receive meals prepared by staff, according to their abilities.
