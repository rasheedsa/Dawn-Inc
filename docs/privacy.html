Privacy

This website does not include a contact form, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or embedded maps. Phone and email links let you contact DAWN using your own applications.

Please do not send medical records, diagnoses or sensitive personal information in an initial email. Contact the intake team to ask how information needed for care planning should be shared.

The hosting provider may process technical request information under its own privacy practices. Opening Google Maps takes you to an external service.

For questions about how DAWN handles information supplied directly to its team, email info@dawnadultwellnessnetwork.com.

If you use the visual website editor, drafts are stored in your browser’s local storage. They are not sent to DAWN or published automatically. You can discard each page’s draft in the editor, or clear this site’s browser data. Download a backup first if you want to keep your edits.
